# Harbour Files sound: licence log

farin-app, 2026-11-16 (Farin: "work on the mood of each game better, give it some lightening in mystery games or some sound effects").
Every file here is **CC0 1.0** (public domain dedication, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), copied unchanged from the Open House set (`../cozy-chores/sfx/`, built and checked by `build-sfx.mjs`, see its LICENSES.md). Credit is not required by CC0; it is kept here anyway.

Everything else Harbour Files plays is made in the engine with WebAudio, with no third-party audio and no AI audio service: the harbour bed (waves = brown noise through a lowpass with a slow swell, the wash = pink noise on the crest, gulls = vibrato sine sweeps, creaking wood = a bandpassed sawtooth with tremolo, a far foghorn = two detuned sawtooths through a lowpass), rain and wind (filtered pink noise), the cafe murmur and cups, the archive clock, the quiet room at home, thunder (a lowpassed noise roll), the reveal stings, the CASE CLOSED chord and the interview room tones (CC12).
