Spanish voice clips in this folder (audio/es)

These MP3 clips were generated with Piper (open-source text-to-speech, https://github.com/rhasspy/piper)
from the Spanish learning content of the Farin Spanish app.

Clips listed in manifest.json with voice "d" ("Daniela"):
  Voice model: es_AR-daniela-high (Piper voices, https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices)
  Trained on: Crowdsourced high-quality Argentinian Spanish speech data set, Google (OpenSLR 61,
  https://www.openslr.org/61/), licensed CC BY-SA 4.0.
  These clips are shared under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence:
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Clips listed in manifest.json with voice "v" ("Dave"):
  Voice model: es_ES-davefx-medium (Piper voices), dataset licensed CC0.

Clips in the folder l/ ("Lucía", the game characters' woman's voice from Spain, 2026-11-23):
  Voice model: es_ES-sharvard-medium, female speaker (Piper voices, https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices)
  Trained on: the Sharvard Corpus, a phonemically balanced Spanish sentence corpus (Aubanel, García Lecumberri and Cooke, 2014;
  https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/574), licensed CC BY 3.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Clips in the folder a/ ("Aldo", the game characters' Mexican man's voice, 2026-11-23):
  Voice model: es_MX-ald-medium (Piper voices), trained on the Ald Mexican Spanish speech dataset, licensed The Unlicense.
